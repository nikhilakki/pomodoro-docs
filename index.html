Built for deep work

Classic Pomodoro loop without the noise of a cloud suite.

Classic cycles

Focus, short break, and long break. Defaults 25 / 5 / 15, long break every four focus sessions.

Tasks that count

Start Focus from a todo. Pomodoros accumulate per task under the dial.

Session history

Local SQLite stats, a seven-day chart, and recent completed or skipped sessions.

Tray and shortcuts

Menu bar controls. Space to start or pause. Command-comma for Settings on macOS.

Your accent

Dial and main button color, or Auto by phase. Light and dark follow the system.

Local-first

Settings, tasks, and history stay on your machine. No account. No cloud.
